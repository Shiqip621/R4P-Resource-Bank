Our approach

More than a list of links.

This prototype maps the infrastructure connecting research and practice: networks, resource hubs, intermediaries and sustained partnerships. It is intended for nonprofit researchers, practitioners, community partners and knowledge brokers.

Each entry explains its functions and why its model may be useful. The collection includes adjacent fields such as education, health and public policy to support learning across sectors.

AccessExchangeConnectionCollaborationCo-production

Use the bank in three steps

  1. Start with your purpose, then narrow by field or geography.
  2. Open a resource’s details to understand its model and potential relevance.
  3. Visit the original source to check current opportunities and participation requirements.

Save a shortlist on this device or export the filtered collection as CSV. Saved items stay in this browser; they are not shared between devices.

Scope: sustained initiatives with an explicit research–practice bridging function. Historical examples are hidden by default. This is an independent working prototype; it does not imply endorsement by ISTR or any listed organization.
