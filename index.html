Our approach

A platform of platforms.

R4P Resource Bank is a platform of platforms: a curated gateway to existing research–practice networks, evidence hubs, knowledge brokers and partnership infrastructures relevant to the nonprofit sector.

It helps researchers and practitioners discover where to go, what each platform offers, whom it serves and how to engage. Resources remain with their original providers; this bank makes a fragmented ecosystem easier to navigate and compare.

Alongside nonprofit and philanthropic initiatives, we include transferable models from community development, education, health, public policy and international development. Related initiatives may share a host; each record maps an identifiable platform or sustained infrastructure.

One shared mapping framework

Basic characteristics: name and host, country and geographic scope, sector, target users and institutional model.

Functions: what the platform makes possible, from dissemination and capacity building to matchmaking and co-production.

Mechanisms: how it operates, such as searchable repositories, communities of practice, training, calls for partners or practitioner research questions.

Knowledge exchange: Research → Practice; Practice → Research; Research ↔ Practice; or Research + Practice → Co-produced Knowledge.

The first 31 records retain their original mapping text alongside provisional new codes. The 20 additions include source links and review dates. Missing tags mean not yet confirmed. This is a working sample, not a comprehensive directory or an endorsement by ISTR or listed organizations.

Saved resources stay in this browser. CSV exports include all matching records and their coded characteristics, even when only part of the results is displayed.
